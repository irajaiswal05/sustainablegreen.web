Catch the
Wind.

Wind is just moving air, but it holds enough power to run the whole world. By building tall turbines, we can turn this invisible breeze into clean electricity day and night.

Ocean Winds

Out at sea, the wind blows much stronger and steadier. These huge ocean farms create massive amounts of power without taking up any land.

Why It Matters

Why the Earth loves wind power.

Choosing wind energy does more than just keep our lights on. It actively protects our natural world, keeps our water clean, and makes our air safe to breathe.

Protecting Lakes & Rivers

It Saves Our Water

Old power plants have to boil millions of gallons of water just to make electricity. Wind turbines do not need a single drop of water to work, which keeps our lakes, rivers, and drinking water full and healthy.

Protecting Our Lungs

Completely Clean Air

Spinning turbine blades do not burn anything, which means they do not make any dirty smoke or smog. Wind power keeps our sky clear blue and the air completely safe for our families to breathe.

Protecting Animals

Sharing the Land

Wind farms take up very little space on the ground. Because the tower only needs a small spot, farmers can still plant crops, and wild animals can roam happily and safely right under the spinning blades.

Global Teamwork

The World is Switching.

More and more countries are building wind farms every single year. As you can see in the chart, the world is working together to choose safe, cheap, and clean power for the planet.
