04 — Power Storage

Endless
Power.

We are moving past the limits of traditional power grids. The future relies on advanced, large-scale energy storage systems that keep power flowing 24/7.

Building the Future of Power.

Making clean energy is only part of the solution. The real challenge is finding clever ways to save, hold, and share that energy across the world without wasting a single drop.

Advanced Solid-State Batteries

We are moving way beyond normal lithium batteries. New solid-state battery designs let us pack huge amounts of power into much smaller spaces while staying completely safe and cool.

Gravity Energy Towers

Using basic physics instead of chemicals. Future power grids will use giant, automated towers and heavy underground weights. When we have extra electricity, we use it to lift heavy blocks, storing energy right in the Earth's gravity.

Molten Salt & Liquid Metal Vaults

Changing how we store massive amounts of energy. By building huge pools of hot melted salt and liquid metals, we can trap sunshine heat for months and turn it back into electricity whenever we need it.

Smart Energy Networks

The power grid of tomorrow won't just be one building. Guided by smart computers, millions of electric cars and smart homes will link together to share and balance power across the world instantly.
