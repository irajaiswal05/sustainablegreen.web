How We Use Solar

Working together with nature and our cities.

Solar energy is more than just panels on a roof. Today, it works side-by-side with farming and powers our biggest cities.

Farming & Solar

Growing Crops Under Panels

By placing solar panels high above farm fields, we can provide shade for crops on hot days. This saves water, helps plants grow better, and keeps the solar panels cool so they create more electricity.

Saves farm water
City Power

Solar Built Into Cities

We are turning glass buildings, windows, and parking lots into power plants. Making electricity right where people live means we don't lose power sending it across long distances.

Power right where we need it
Massive Scale

Huge Desert Solar Farms

In hot, sunny deserts, massive solar farms track the sun as it moves across the sky. These giant fields of panels create enough power to run whole factories and cities without burning any coal or gas.

Panels move to follow the sun
Protecting Our Planet

Why we need to switch to solar now.

Moving to solar is about saving our environment. The chart below shows just how much pollution we can stop by choosing solar energy over dirty fossil fuels like coal and gas.

The Big Advantage

Solar runs on free, endless sunlight. We don't need to dig holes in the earth for fuel, and we don't need to transport it across the ocean. Once it is built, the energy is practically free.

The Global Need

Our climate is changing fast because of the pollution we put into the air. We need to replace dirty energy with clean solar power immediately to protect the earth for the future.

Healing the Earth

Solar power helps stop mountains from being mined, keeps dirty chemicals out of our drinking water, and stops harmful smoke from polluting the air we breathe.
